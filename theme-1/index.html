The Atelier Standards

Rooted in Craftsmanship

Every garment is crafted slowly in limited batches, honoring master artisans and sustainable heritage traditions.

01

Pure Heritage Fibers

Exclusively handspun mulberry silks, certified organic cotton mulmul, and hand-loomed Maheshwari weaves that breathe naturally with the body.

02

Master Artisan Tailoring

Handcrafted across multi-generational karigar clusters in Lucknow, Chanderi, and Bengal. Fair living wages and revered artisanal lineage.

03

Made-to-Measure Fits

We celebrate individual posture and proportions. Complimentary custom tailoring and length adjustments available for every patron.

04

Conscious Luxury

100% plastic-free, recyclable presentation boxes with hand-stitched cotton garment pouches and carbon-neutral express dispatch.
